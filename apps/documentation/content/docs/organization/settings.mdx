# Organization Settings

Organization settings allow you to manage your organization's details, preferences, and configuration options.

## Accessing Settings

To access your organization settings:

1. Navigate to the **Organization** section in the sidebar
2. Click on **Settings**
3. You'll see the settings form with your current information

## Settings Overview

### Organization Details

#### Name

- The official name of your organization
- Visible to all team members and participants
- Can be changed at any time

#### Slug

- URL-friendly identifier for your organization
- Used in some URLs and references
- Auto-generated from the name but can be customized

### Contact Information

#### Email

- Primary contact email for the organization
- Used for important notifications
- Should be monitored regularly

#### Phone (Optional)

- Contact phone number
- Useful for urgent communications

### Address Information

#### Location

- Physical address of your organization
- Used for carbon footprint calculations
- Important for project location context

#### Country

- The country where your organization is based
- Affects timezone and regional settings
- Used in reporting and compliance

## Managing Settings

### Editing Organization Details

1. Navigate to **Settings**
2. Modify the fields you want to change
3. Click **"Save Changes"**
4. Changes take effect immediately

### Validation

The settings form validates:

- Required fields are filled
- Email format is correct
- Phone numbers are valid
- Names are not empty

## Advanced Settings

### Timezone

Set your organization's default timezone:

- Affects project dates and times
- Used for reporting periods
- Can be overridden per project if needed

### Language

Default language for your organization:

- Affects system notifications
- Used for participant communications
- Currently supports: English, German, and more

### Branding (Future)

Planned features include:

- Custom logo upload
- Color scheme customization
- Branded participant forms

## Permissions

### Who Can Access Settings

| Role                       | View Settings | Edit Settings |
| -------------------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Organization Administrator | ✅            | ✅            |
| Project Coordinator        | ✅            | ❌            |
| Participant                | ❌            | ❌            |

Only **Organization Administrators** can modify organization settings. Project Coordinators can view settings but cannot make changes.

## Danger Zone

### Delete Organization

**Warning**: This action is irreversible.

To delete your organization:

1. Go to Settings
2. Scroll to the "Danger Zone" section
3. Click **"Delete Organization"**
4. Confirm the deletion

**What gets deleted:**

- All organization data
- All projects and their data
- All participant information
- Team member associations

**What to do before deleting:**

- Export any important data
- Notify team members
- Complete or transfer active projects

## Troubleshooting

### Changes Not Saving

If your settings changes don't persist:

- Check for validation errors
- Ensure you clicked "Save Changes"
- Verify you have Organization Administrator permissions
- Try refreshing the page

### Invalid Email Error

- Ensure the email format is correct (e.g., name@domain.com)
- Check for typos or extra spaces
- Use a valid, accessible email address

### Slug Already Taken

If your preferred slug is unavailable:

- Add a location or department identifier
- Use an abbreviation
- Contact support if you believe there's a conflict

## Best Practices

### Keeping Information Current

- **Update annually** - Review settings at least once a year
- **After relocations** - Update address if your organization moves
- **Contact changes** - Keep email and phone current
- **Rebranding** - Update name and details if your organization rebrands

### Security Considerations

- **Use organization email** - Not a personal email address
- **Monitor access** - Only Organization Administrators should have settings access
- **Regular audits** - Review who has Organization Administrator permissions
