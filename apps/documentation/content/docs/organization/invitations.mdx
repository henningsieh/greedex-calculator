# Team Invitations

The invitation system allows you to add new members to your organization. This covers both sending invitations and accepting them.

## Sending Invitations

### Who Can Invite

- **Organization Administrators** can invite members with any role (Organization Administrator, Project Coordinator, Participant)
- **Project Coordinators** can only invite Participants

### How to Send an Invitation

1. Go to the **Team** page (`/org/team`)
2. Click the **"Invite Member"** button
3. Fill in the invitation form:
   - **Email** - The invitee's email address
   - **Name** - The invitee's full name
   - **Role** - Select the appropriate role (Organization Administrator, Project Coordinator, or Participant)
4. Click **"Send Invitation"**

### What Happens Next

- The invitee receives an email with an invitation link
- The invitation appears in the team list as "Pending"
- The invitee must accept the invitation to join

## Accepting Invitations

### For New Users

If the invitee doesn't have a Greendex account:

1. Click the invitation link in the email
2. You'll be redirected to the signup page
3. Create an account
4. After verification, you'll automatically join the organization

### For Existing Users

If the invitee already has a Greendex account:

1. Click the invitation link in the email
2. Log in if prompted
3. Click **"Accept Invitation"** on the acceptance page
4. You'll be added to the organization and redirected to the dashboard

## Managing Invitations

### Viewing Pending Invitations

Pending invitations are displayed in the Team page with a "Pending" status.

### Canceling Invitations

Organization Administrators can cancel pending invitations from the Team page.

## Troubleshooting

### Invitation Not Received

- Check spam/junk folders
- Verify the email address was entered correctly
- Try resending the invitation

### Invitation Link Expired

Contact an organization owner to send a new invitation.
