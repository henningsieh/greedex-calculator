# Team Management

The Team page allows you to view and manage your organization's team members - Owners and Employees who help manage the organization and its projects.

## Understanding Team Roles

### Owner

**Full administrative control:**

- Can invite members with any role
- Can delete the organization
- Can manage all projects and settings
- Can remove any team member
- Can transfer ownership

**Best for:** Organization administrators, project coordinators

### Employee

**Project management access:**

- Can create and manage projects
- Can invite Participants
- Can view team and participant lists
- Cannot delete the organization
- Cannot invite Owners or Employees

**Best for:** Project managers, coordinators, support staff

### Participant

**Limited project access:**

- Can participate in assigned projects
- Can submit carbon footprint data
- Cannot invite other members
- Cannot access team management

**Best for:** Project participants, volunteers

## Viewing Your Team

### Team List

The Team page displays:

- **Name** - Team member's full name
- **Email** - Contact email address
- **Role** - Current role in the organization
- **Status** - Active or pending invitation
- **Joined** - When they became a member

### Filtering and Sorting

Use the table controls to:

- **Search** - Find members by name or email
- **Filter by role** - Show only Owners, Employees, or all
- **Sort** - Order by name, role, or join date
- **Pagination** - Navigate through large teams

## Managing Team Members

### Inviting New Members

See the [Invitations](/docs/organization/invitations) page for detailed instructions on inviting team members.

Quick overview:

1. Click **"Invite Member"**
2. Enter email and name
3. Select role
4. Send invitation

### Viewing Pending Invitations

Pending invitations appear in the team list with:

- "Pending" status badge
- Invitation date
- Role they were invited as

### Canceling Invitations

To cancel a pending invitation:

1. Find the pending member in the list
2. Click the **"Cancel"** button
3. Confirm the cancellation

The invitee will no longer be able to accept the invitation.

## Role Management

### Changing Roles

Owners can change member roles:

1. Find the team member in the list
2. Click on their current role
3. Select the new role from the dropdown
4. Confirm the change

### Role Change Permissions

| Action                | Owner | Employee | Participant |
| --------------------- | ----- | -------- | ----------- |
| Promote to Owner      | ✅    | ❌       | ❌          |
| Demote Owner          | ✅    | ❌       | ❌          |
| Change to Employee    | ✅    | ❌       | ❌          |
| Change to Participant | ✅    | ✅\*     | ❌          |

\*Employees can only change Participants to other roles

### Transferring Ownership

To transfer organization ownership:

1. Go to Team page
2. Find the member to promote
3. Change their role to **Owner**
4. (Optional) Demote yourself to Employee

**Note:** An organization must always have at least one Owner.

## Removing Team Members

### Removing a Member

Owners can remove team members:

1. Find the member in the team list
2. Click the **"Remove"** button
3. Confirm the removal

**What happens when removed:**

- Member loses access to the organization
- Their participant data remains in projects
- They can be re-invited later
- Their account remains active for other organizations

### Cannot Remove Yourself

You cannot remove yourself from the organization:

- Ask another Owner to remove you
- Or delete your account entirely

## Team Size Limits

### Current Limits

- **No hard limit** on team size
- Practical limits based on your plan
- Contact support for large organization needs

### Best Practices

- **Regular audits** - Review team membership quarterly
- **Principle of least privilege** - Give minimum necessary access
- **Remove inactive** - Clean up members who no longer participate
- **Document roles** - Keep track of who does what

## Troubleshooting

### Member Not Showing

If a team member doesn't appear:

- Check if they're in the Participants list instead
- Verify the invitation was accepted
- Ensure you're viewing the correct organization
- Check filters aren't hiding them

### Can't Change Role

If you can't change a member's role:

- Verify you have Owner permissions
- Check if they're the only Owner (must have at least one)
- Ensure the member has accepted their invitation

### Invitation Issues

For invitation problems, see [Invitations Troubleshooting](/docs/organization/invitations#troubleshooting).

## Security Best Practices

### Access Control

- **Limit Owners** - Only trusted administrators should be Owners
- **Regular reviews** - Audit roles quarterly
- **Offboarding** - Remove access when team members leave
- **Monitor invites** - Track pending invitations

### Sensitive Operations

Only Owners can:

- Delete the organization
- Access billing information
- Change critical settings
- Remove other Owners
