# Organization

Organizations are the top-level entity in Greendex. Each organization represents a legal entity (company, NGO, institution) that runs Erasmus+ projects.

## Organization Structure

An organization contains:

- **Team Members** - People who manage the organization
- **Projects** - Erasmus+ activities being tracked
- **Participants** - People who take part in projects
- **Settings** - Organization configuration

## Member Roles

Greendex uses a role-based access system:

### Owner

- Full control over the organization
- Can invite members with any role
- Can delete the organization
- Can manage all projects and settings

### Employee

- Can create and manage projects
- Can invite participants
- Can view team and participant lists
- Cannot delete the organization

### Participant

- Limited access to projects they participate in
- Cannot invite other members
- Can submit their carbon footprint data
