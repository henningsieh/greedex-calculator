# Email Verification

Email verification is a required step to activate your Greendex account and ensure the security of your organization data.

## Why Verification is Required

Email verification helps:

- **Confirm your identity** - Ensures you own the email address
- **Secure your account** - Prevents unauthorized access
- **Enable communication** - Allows us to send important notifications
- **Protect your data** - Adds a layer of security to your organization

## The Verification Process

### After Sign Up

When you create a new Greendex account:

1. **Check your inbox** - Look for an email from Greendex
2. **Open the verification email** - Subject: "Verify your email address"
3. **Click the verification link** - This confirms your email
4. **Account activated** - You're ready to use Greendex

### Verification Email Contents

The email contains:

- A personalized greeting
- A **"Verify Email"** button/link
- Information about what happens next
- Support contact information

## Resending Verification Email

If you didn't receive the verification email or it expired:

### From the Verify Email Page

1. You'll see the verify email page immediately after signup
2. Your email address is displayed on the page
3. Click **"Resend Email"** to request a new verification link

### Cooldown Period

For security reasons:

- You can only resend the email every 60 seconds
- A countdown timer shows when you can resend
- Attempting to resend during cooldown shows a warning

### If Email is Missing

Check these common issues:

| Issue               | Solution                                   |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Spam/Junk folder    | Check your email's spam or junk folder     |
| Wrong email address | Go back and check the email you entered    |
| Email delay         | Wait a few minutes for delivery            |
| Corporate filters   | Contact your IT department about whitelist |

## Verification Link Behavior

### Successful Verification

When you click a valid verification link:

- Your email is marked as verified
- Your account becomes fully active
- You're redirected to the login page
- You can now log in and create an organization

### Invalid or Expired Links

If the verification link doesn't work:

- **Expired** - Links expire after a set time for security
- **Already used** - Each link can only be used once
- **Wrong email** - The link is tied to a specific email address

**Solution**: Request a new verification email from the verify email page.

## Troubleshooting

### No Email Received

1. **Wait 5 minutes** - Sometimes delivery takes time
2. **Check all folders** - Including spam, junk, promotions
3. **Verify email address** - Ensure you typed it correctly during signup
4. **Try resending** - Use the resend button after the cooldown
5. **Contact support** - If issues persist

### "Email Already Verified"

If you see this message:

- Your email is already verified
- You can proceed to log in
- No further action needed

### Can't Access Email Account

If you lose access to the email you signed up with:

- Contact your organization administrator
- May need to create a new account with a different email
- Update email in user settings if you regain access

## After Verification

Once your email is verified:

1. **Log in** - Use your credentials to access Greendex
2. **Create organization** - Set up your first organization
3. **Invite team** - Add colleagues to help manage projects
4. **Start tracking** - Begin creating projects and tracking carbon footprint

## Security Notes

### Email Verification Best Practices

- **Use a personal email** - One you have long-term access to
- **Keep email secure** - Protect the email account with strong passwords
- **Update if needed** - Change your email in settings if circumstances change
- **Watch for phishing** - Only click links from official Greendex emails

### Recognizing Legitimate Emails

Genuine Greendex emails:

- Come from official Greendex domains
- Include your name and signup details
- Never ask for your password
- Have professional formatting
