# Sign Up

Create a new Greendex account to start tracking your organization's carbon footprint.

## Registration Process

1. Visit the signup page
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a secure password
4. Accept the terms of service
5. Click "Create Account"

## Email Verification

After registration:

1. Check your email for a verification link
2. Click the link to verify your email
3. Your account will be activated

## What Happens Next

After creating your account, you'll be prompted to:

1. Create your first organization
2. Set up your team
3. Create your first project

## Already Have an Account?

If you already have an account, [log in here](/docs/authentication/login).
