# Login

Access your Greendex account to manage your organization and projects.

## How to Log In

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Sign In"

## Social Login

You can also log in using:

- Google
- GitHub

## Troubleshooting

### Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your password, click "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions to reset it.

### Account Not Verified

If you haven't verified your email address, you'll need to complete verification before logging in.

## Next Steps

After logging in, you'll be redirected to:

- Your organization's dashboard (if you have an organization)
- The organization creation page (if you don't have an organization yet)
