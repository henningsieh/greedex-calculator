# Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your Greendex account password, you can easily reset it through our secure password recovery process.

## Requesting a Password Reset

### Step 1: Access the Forgot Password Page

1. Go to the [Login](/docs/authentication/login) page
2. Click on the **"Forgot Password?"** link below the login form
3. You'll be redirected to the password reset request page

### Step 2: Enter Your Email

1. Enter the email address associated with your Greendex account
2. Click **"Send Reset Link"**
3. A confirmation message will appear

### Step 3: Check Your Email

1. Open your email inbox
2. Look for an email from Greendex with the subject "Password Reset"
3. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam/junk folder

### Step 4: Click the Reset Link

1. Open the password reset email
2. Click the **"Reset Password"** button or link
3. You'll be redirected to the password reset page

## Resetting Your Password

Once you click the reset link, you'll need to:

1. **Enter a new password** - Must be at least 6 characters long
2. **Confirm the new password** - Re-enter to ensure it matches
3. **Click "Reset Password"** - Your password will be updated

After successful reset:

- You'll see a confirmation message
- You'll be redirected to the login page
- Use your new password to log in

## Security Considerations

### Password Requirements

Your new password must:

- Be at least 6 characters long
- Not be the same as your previous password
- Be unique and not used on other sites

### Reset Link Expiration

Password reset links expire after a limited time for security reasons. If your link has expired:

- Request a new reset link
- The old link will no longer work

## Troubleshooting

### Didn't Receive the Email?

- **Check spam/junk folders** - The email may have been filtered
- **Verify your email address** - Ensure you entered it correctly
- **Wait a few minutes** - Email delivery can sometimes be delayed
- **Try again** - Request another reset link if needed

### Reset Link Not Working?

- **Link expired** - Request a new reset link
- **Already used** - Each link can only be used once
- **Wrong email** - Ensure you're using the correct email address

### Still Having Issues?

Contact your organization administrator or support for assistance with account recovery.
